Coordinated vulnerability disclosure — eustatement
===================================================

This platform holds end-user statements for restricted chemicals, which means
it holds buyers' names, addresses and identity document numbers. We would
rather hear about a weakness from you than from the people whose data it is.

What we ask
-----------

- Report it to security@eustatement.example before telling anyone else, and
  give us a reasonable period to fix it.
- Do not use more access than you need to demonstrate the problem. If you reach
  personal data, stop, tell us what you reached, and do not keep a copy.
- No denial-of-service testing, no spam, no social engineering of our staff or
  our customers, and no physical attempts.
- Do not test against the third-party services this platform depends on — the
  identity verification provider, the EU VIES register or PubChem. They are not
  ours to authorise, and traffic at them is billed to us or blocks our address.
  Say so in your report and we will reproduce it ourselves.

What you can expect
-------------------

- An acknowledgement within three working days.
- An assessment, and a date by which we expect to have it fixed.
- Credit in our release notes if you want it, and silence if you prefer.
- No legal action against anyone who follows this policy in good faith.

What is in scope
----------------

The web application, its API, and the embeddable form. Reports about missing
security headers on static assets, or about software versions without a
demonstrated exploit, are welcome but will usually be closed as informational.

Two things we already know and have decided about, so they are not findings:
the email addresses of seller accounts are stored unencrypted, and a public
form's address can be discovered by trying slugs. Both are written up in
SECURITY.md with the reasoning.
